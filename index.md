## Featured Projects

### 1. Formula 1 Driver Performance Analysis — 2024

**Research question:** How did Formula 1 drivers perform during the 2024 season, based on championship points, wins, podiums, and finishing positions?

**Methods:** Python, Pandas, public racing data, and Matplotlib.

**Key visualization:** A comparison of the top drivers by championship points and average finishing position.

**Project repository:** https://github.com/JIANGRUOXUN/data-science-project

### 2. San Jose Sharks Season Analysis — 2024–25

**Research question:** How did the San Jose Sharks perform throughout the 2024–25 NHL regular season, and how did their results vary over time?

**Methods:** Python, Pandas, public NHL data, and Matplotlib.

**Key visualization:** Game-by-game goals scored and conceded, along with monthly win rates.

**Project repository:** https://github.com/JIANGRUOXUN/data-science-project
